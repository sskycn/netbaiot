## 2. Scope of Contributions

A "Contribution" is source code, a patch, documentation, a test or other original
material intentionally submitted by the Contributor for inclusion in or
improvement of NetbaIoT through a pull request or another channel expressly
designated by the Maintainer, including a designated version control system.

This Agreement covers Contributions submitted after its effective date.
Contributions submitted earlier are covered only when both parties expressly
agree to include them in an attachment identifying the relevant submissions.

Future Contributions are covered only when intentionally submitted for NetbaIoT
by the signing Contributor, or by an authorized submitter on behalf of a signing
organization, within the scope recorded under Section 10 and with the authority
required by Section 7. A different rights holder is not automatically covered by
an earlier signature. The Maintainer must link covered submissions to the relevant
signing record through repository, pull request or other submission references,
commit identifiers and the submitter's identity, as applicable.

Material clearly marked "Not a Contribution", or provided only for discussion or
evaluation without permission for inclusion, is excluded. Ordinary discussions
and issue reports are not automatically Contributions; any original material in
them must be expressly designated for inclusion and satisfy the conditions above
to be covered. Third-party material is subject to Section 7, not automatically
included in the grants.

## 4. Continued availability of an open source version

The Maintainer undertakes to continue making an open source version of NetbaIoT
publicly available while conducting commercial licensing under this Agreement.

For a Contribution covered by this Agreement, accepted for NetbaIoT and used in
commercial licensing, the Maintainer must continue to provide at least one open
source version containing that Contribution and its source code under
`AGPL-3.0-or-later`. Contributions removed from the latest version through
refactoring or removal may remain available in a public archived version.

This Section does not require feature parity between open source and commercial
versions, or require all independent additions or subsequent modifications in a
commercial version to be open sourced.

Continued source availability is not a commitment to free technical support,
maintenance, defect repairs, upgrades or a particular release schedule.

The Maintainer may withhold, redact or remove specific material only to the extent
reasonably necessary to comply with law or a binding order, respect third-party
rights, address an intellectual property dispute supported by reasonable grounds,
prevent disclosure of confidential, personal or other sensitive information, or
address a substantial security risk supported by reasonable grounds. The
Maintainer must retain a record of the reason and scope, keep unaffected material
available, and restore a lawful, safe version when reasonably feasible. Public
notice of a removal must be given where lawful and safe, without disclosing the
protected information.

These exceptions do not waive obligations under the AGPL or other applicable
licenses. If continued distribution cannot comply with those obligations and the
applicable legal constraints, the affected work must not be distributed on
incompatible terms. Stopping the Maintainer's own publication of material does
not revoke open source rights already lawfully granted or create new restrictions
on their exercise.

Commercial licensing does not revoke or restrict open source licenses already
lawfully granted. Individuals and organizations may use, modify, deploy and
redistribute the open source version, including for commercial purposes, without
signing this Agreement or purchasing a commercial license, provided they comply
with the AGPL. Rights under the AGPL, including its own termination and
reinstatement rules, remain governed by that license.

The Contributor grants the Maintainer a worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free
patent license only for claims the Contributor owns, controls or is otherwise
entitled to license. The license covers the remaining duration of the relevant
patents, with the right to sublicense to its license recipients through multiple
tiers within the scope of this Section. The
Contributor also grants each lawful recipient of a covered Contribution,
including commercial customers and lawful downstream recipients, the same patent
license directly to exercise their rights in that Contribution under their
applicable open source or commercial license. These grants are irrevocable except
for the termination described below or as required by mandatory applicable law.

The grants cover only claims that, without a license, would necessarily be
infringed by a Contribution itself or by its combination with NetbaIoT as it
existed when that Contribution was submitted. The submission record must identify
that project revision where applicable. They permit making, having made, using,
selling, offering for sale, importing and other acts requiring permission under
those claims. Later combinations or modifications do not expand the covered
claims. The Contributor does not represent that it owns or can license patents
held by third parties, or that all possible uses are free of patent infringement.

If a recipient initiates a lawsuit alleging that the covered Contribution or
combination infringes a patent owned or controlled by that recipient, the
Contributor may, to the extent permitted by applicable law, terminate only that
recipient's patent permissions under this Section for the Contribution or
combination at issue. Termination requires written notice identifying the lawsuit,
affected permission and effective date, which must not precede receipt of the
notice. Unrelated patent disputes do not trigger this mechanism; other recipients,
including downstream recipients, retain their permissions unless they themselves
satisfy the trigger. The copyright grant is unaffected. Independently arising
rights and obligations under the AGPL or another license remain governed by their
own terms, not altered by this mechanism.

**Legal review before adoption:** Finalize the treatment of patent counterclaims,

## 7. Authority and third-party material

The Contributor represents that the Contributor is entitled to enter into this
Agreement and grant the rights stated in it, both when signing and when submitting
each covered Contribution.

If an employer, client or other organization has rights in a Contribution, the
Contributor must obtain authorization covering this Agreement's grants or arrange
for the relevant rights holder to sign a separate agreement. An individual's
signature does not replace authorization required from an organization.

The Contributor must distinguish between:

- original material in which the Contributor holds the rights being granted;
- material in which an employer, client or other organization may hold rights;
- modifications of third-party open source material, identifying the underlying
  work separately from the Contributor's own additions;
- material originating from other contributors, identifying its actual rights
  holders and applicable permissions; and
- proprietary material, which must not be submitted without permission for the
  intended disclosure and use.

The Contributor must identify the source, licenses and known restrictions of
third-party material in a submission and retain required notices. **This
Agreement does not expand the Contributor's authority over third-party material
or automatically make that material eligible for commercial sublicensing.**

Permission to include material in an AGPL project does not, by itself, permit
closed source commercial relicensing. Such use requires separate authorization or
an existing license that permits the intended use, subject to its conditions. The
Maintainer must review those permissions before treating third-party material as
eligible for commercial licensing. Only the rights the Contributor can actually
grant are covered by this Agreement.

The Contributor must not knowingly submit trade secrets or confidential material
that the Contributor is not entitled to disclose. The Contributor must promptly
notify the Maintainer upon learning that a rights representation is inaccurate or
that a relevant rights dispute exists.

The Maintainer may decide whether to accept, modify, publish or remove a

Except for express representations in this Agreement and mandatory legal
requirements, Contributions are provided "as is". The Contributor has no ongoing
maintenance, support, repair or delivery obligation and gives no warranty of
merchantability or fitness for a particular purpose.

The "as is" provision does not negate the express authority and disclosure
representations in Section 7. It does not create an additional guarantee that no
third-party intellectual property claim can arise.

This Agreement creates no employment, partnership or agency relationship and no
entitlement to a share of revenue.

## 9. Project succession

Subject to applicable law, the Maintainer may transfer its rights and obligations
under this Agreement with a transfer of NetbaIoT or its related business. This may
include forming a company and transferring the project to it, restructuring,
merger, a project or business sale, or lawful succession to project rights.

The successor must be an identified individual or organization with legal capacity
to hold the rights and assume the obligations. It must assume all transferred
obligations, including Section 4, through a written undertaking or legally
effective succession evidence. The Maintainer or successor, as applicable, must
provide timely public notice identifying the successor and its contact details,
and notify Contributors through their recorded contact channel where reasonably
practicable.

The Contributor consents in advance to a project-related transfer meeting these
conditions only to the extent that such consent is legally effective. Any further
consent, notice or formality required by applicable law must be satisfied before
the contractual transfer takes effect. This Section does not make every transfer
unconditionally or automatically effective, or authorize transfer of rights
outside this Agreement.

Such a transfer does not affect the Contributor's retained rights or existing
licenses.

## 10. Effective date, future Contributions and amendments

This Agreement takes effect when the Contributor expressly signs it and the
Maintainer receives the signing record. A verifiable record must be retained for
either a paper or electronic signature.

The Maintainer must retain a verifiable signing record containing:

- the Contributor's legal identity, GitHub account and contact email;
- whether the Contributor signs personally or on behalf of an organization,
  identifying that organization and its representative's authority as applicable;
- the exact agreement version and an immutable copy of both language texts, with
  a repository revision or content digest identifying the signed texts;
- the signing timestamp with time zone and the express consent record;
- the project and scope of covered Contributions, including records linking future
  submissions to the signer and any expressly agreed historical attachment;
- the controlling language agreed by both parties; and
- the Maintainer's acknowledgment of receipt and its date.

A pull request, contribution submission or Git `Signed-off-by` line does not by
itself constitute signing this Agreement or consent to commercial sublicensing.
The grants apply to covered future submissions without requiring a new signature
for each submission. A change of rights holder or signing capacity requires a new
or supplemental authorization record before the affected Contributions are treated
as covered; it does not by itself revoke earlier valid grants.

The Contributor may stop submitting future Contributions. Stopping
Contributions, leaving the project or closing an account does not revoke grants
already effective, except as required by mandatory applicable law or under the
patent termination provision in Section 5.

An amendment applies to a Contributor only with that Contributor's express
agreement. Publishing a new version in the repository alone does not constitute
acceptance of an amendment. Contributions covered by an earlier signed version
remain governed by that version unless the Contributor expressly agrees otherwise.
